Quick Kick (AUG-1002)

DE: Quick-Kick

Ein extrem kurzer, präziser Prompt mit sofortiger Wirkung auf Motivation, Fokus oder Entscheidungsfähigkeit. Kein Tiefgang, sondern Startenergie.

Operational Definition

Quick Kick is observed when brief sub-60-second AI-interactions produce a measurable mood-lift in the participant, evidenced by positive-affect increase on a state-affect scale immediately following the brief interaction.

Measurement Schema

Kendall tau correlation between interaction-duration (in seconds, capped at 60s) and a single-item visual-analogue mood-lift score administered 60 seconds post-interaction across n >= 40 brief sessions. Quick Kick effectiveness = tau effect-size paired with median mood-lift magnitude.

Inclusion criteria

Interaction duration < 90 seconds AND mood-lift VAS administered post-interaction.

Exclusion criteria

Sessions with multiple interactions within the 60-second post-window.
