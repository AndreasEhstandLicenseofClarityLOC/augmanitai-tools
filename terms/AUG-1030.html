Oops Moment (AUG-1030)

DE: Hups-Moment

Der bewusst akzeptierte Fehltritt der KI, der als Lernsignal genutzt wird. Kein Ärger, sondern schnelle Korrektur und Weitergehen.

Operational Definition

An Oops Moment is observed when participants explicitly mark a failure or error in AI output with playful (rather than frustrated) framing, evidenced by playful-error markers (lol, haha, oops, oh no, oh dear) within two turns of the error.

Measurement Schema

Goal Orientation scale (mastery vs performance, 8 items) administered alongside utterance-lexicon coding of error-framing (playful vs frustrated). Oops-Moment rate = playful-coded errors / total recognised errors, with mastery-goal-orientation hypothesised as positive predictor in logistic regression.

Exclusion criteria
