The Seasonal User (AUG-0305)

A user whose AI intensity fluctuates depending on life phase, project load, or season — sometimes intensive daily use, sometimes weeks-long pauses. Describes the observation that AI use need not be a linear process. Related to AUG-0141 (The Spectrum Framework), AUG-0120 (The Range Framework), and AUG-0316 (The Own Pace).

Operational Definition

The Seasonal User pattern is observed when individual usage rhythms exhibit periodic intensification and dormancy tied to external temporal anchors (work-cycles, academic terms, project-deadlines) rather than uniform usage.

Measurement Schema

Spectral analysis (Fast Fourier Transform) of daily session-count time-series over >= 180 days, with peak-frequency identification. Seasonality detected when power-spectrum peaks correspond to frequencies of 1/7-day, 1/30-day, or 1/90-day with significance via permutation test.

Inclusion criteria

Minimum 180 days of daily usage data with at least one significant FFT peak.

Exclusion criteria

Time-series with > 25% missing days or single-session bursts only.
