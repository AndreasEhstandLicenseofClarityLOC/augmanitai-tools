The Hidden Angle Finder (AUG-0534)

The use of AI to discover a previously unconsidered perspective in a familiar topic — the hidden angle the user would not have found alone. Related to AUG-0248 (The Surprise Angle), AUG-0040 (Perspective Triangulation), and AUG-0225 (The Unexpected Voice).

Operational Definition

The Hidden Angle Finder is observed when AI-assisted analysis surfaces non-obvious perspectives on a problem that the participant did not anticipate in their initial framing, evidenced by explicit acknowledgment-of-novel-angle markers in subsequent turns.

Measurement Schema

Latent growth curve modeling applied to participant's perspective-count over a session, with random-intercept and random-slope. Hidden-Angle events = positive deviations from individual growth-trajectory exceeding 1 SD residual, paired with verbal acknowledgment.
