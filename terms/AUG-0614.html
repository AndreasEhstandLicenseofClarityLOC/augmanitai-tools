The Synthetic Spotting (AUG-0614)

The ability to recognize AI-generated content — through stylistic features, typical formulation patterns, or content indicators. Related to AUG-0378 (The Turing Suspicion), AUG-0452 (The Reality Blur), and AUG-0088 (Algorithmic Intuition).

Operational Definition

The Synthetic Spotting capacity is observed when participants accurately identify AI-generated content in mixed-provenance text-sets across multiple domains, measured via forced-choice classification accuracy above chance.

Measurement Schema

Item Response Theory (IRT) 2PL model fitted to participant-by-item accuracy matrix, with discrimination parameter a and difficulty parameter b estimated per item. Spotting-ability theta reported per participant on the standard IRT scale.
