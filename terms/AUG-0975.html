The Vigilance Rhythm (AUG-0975)

DE: Der Aufmerksamkeits-Rhythmus

The observation that sustained AI-output verification benefits from a paced cadence: deliberate verification at chosen checkpoints rather than continuous monitoring. A rhythm of focused review and trusted intervals preserves attention quality over longer interactions.

Operational Definition

The Vigilance Rhythm is observed when participants display cyclic patterns of high and low verification-effort across sessions, evidenced by alternation between verification-intensive and verification-sparse phases over time.

Measurement Schema

Friedman test (non-parametric repeated-measures) applied to verification-rate across consecutive session-blocks (3-session blocks). Significant Friedman chi-square indicates non-random rhythmic variation; effect-size W reported.

Inclusion criteria

At least 30 sessions to support 10 or more blocks.

Exclusion criteria

Participants with monotonic increase or decrease in verification (no rhythm).
