The Everyday Insight (AUG-0625)

An insight gained through AI interaction that changes the user in everyday life — a new perspective on an everyday phenomenon, a surprising connection, or a helpful reinterpretation. Related to AUG-0292 (The View Shift), AUG-0248 (The Surprise Angle), and AUG-0054 (Augmented Understanding).

Operational Definition

An Everyday Insight is recorded when a participant reports a novel realization arising from a routine AI interaction (cooking, scheduling, casual question) within 24 hours of the exchange, including a verbal description of (a) the trigger turn, (b) the realization content, and (c) the application context.

Measurement Schema

Diary-study with daily prompts over 14 days: free-text insight entries scored by two raters on a 0-3 novelty rubric (0=no insight, 1=restatement, 2=connection, 3=novel reframing). Frequency = insights per day; depth = mean novelty score.

Inclusion criteria

Diary entry within 24h linking a routine AI exchange to a stated realization.

Exclusion criteria

Insights from explicit problem-solving sessions or task-instructed reflection.
