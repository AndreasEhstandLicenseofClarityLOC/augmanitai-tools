Relaxation Edit (AUG-1014)

DE: Entspannungs-Überarbeitung

Das bewusste Nachbearbeiten eines Textes oder Gesprächs mit dem Ziel, Druck, Härte oder Überforderung sprachlich zu reduzieren.

Operational Definition

A Relaxation Edit is observed when participants intentionally use AI to soften or de-intensify a piece of their own writing, characterised by explicit relax-request prompts and downstream lexical-intensity reduction in output.

Measurement Schema

Big Five personality (NEO-PI-R) Neuroticism facet administered alongside lexical-intensity scoring (VADER-intensity or LIWC affect-intensity) on pre- and post-edit text. Relaxation-Edit effectiveness = intensity-reduction modulated by Neuroticism subscore.

Inclusion criteria

Explicit soften / relax / tone-down request AND NEO-PI-R Neuroticism facet completed.

Exclusion criteria

Edits requested in third-person context (editing someone else's text).
