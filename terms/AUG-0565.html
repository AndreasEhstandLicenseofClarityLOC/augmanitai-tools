The Balance Filter (AUG-0565)

The conscious strategy of balancing AI use and non-digital activities — through fixed times, rules, or routines that ensure AI enriches everyday life rather than dominating it. Related to AUG-0074 (Analog Anchors), AUG-0282 (The Dinner Table Pause), and Axiom 7 (The Return Principle).

Operational Definition

The Balance Filter is observed when participants explicitly request balanced multi-perspective output from AI on a contested topic, evidenced by requests containing balance-markers (both sides, multiple perspectives, pros and cons, fair) paired with downstream evaluation of received balance.

Measurement Schema

Bayesian factor analysis on participant ratings of received output across 5 balance-dimensions (perspective-count, weight-symmetry, citation-balance, tonal-neutrality, missing-perspectives-flagged). Bayes-Factor > 3 against single-factor model indicates multidimensional balance perception.
