Feel-Good Warm (AUG-1013)

DE: Wohlfühl-Wärme

Ein emotional unterstützender Interaktionsstil, der Sicherheit, Zugehörigkeit und Akzeptanz vermittelt – ohne Problemlösung zu erzwingen.

Operational Definition

Feel-Good Warm is observed when AI-mediated interactions produce a sustained low-arousal positive-affect state in the participant, characterised by warm-positive affect markers persisting for >= 15 minutes post-interaction.

Measurement Schema

Schwartz Values Survey administered alongside intra-class correlation (ICC 3,1) on a 6-item warm-affect single-item-set sampled at three post-interaction timepoints (immediate, 15-min, 30-min). Sustained warmth detected when ICC > 0.7 AND value-orientation scores point to benevolence > self-direction.

Inclusion criteria

All three post-interaction warm-affect probes completed AND Schwartz Values Survey within 30 days.

Exclusion criteria

Participants reporting concurrent mood-altering substances within the warmth-window.
