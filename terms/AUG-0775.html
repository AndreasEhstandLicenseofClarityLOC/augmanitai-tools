The KI-Free Zone (AUG-0775)

The conscious establishment of areas — spatial, temporal, or thematic — in which AI use is excluded. Related to AUG-0773 (The Conscious Refusal), AUG-0632 (The Offline Moment), and AUG-0565 (The Balance Filter).

Operational Definition

The KI-Free Zone is observed when participants deliberately demarcate temporal, spatial, or topical contexts as off-limits for AI consultation, with the demarcation explicitly stated and adhered-to across the observation window.

Measurement Schema

Moderation analysis with interaction term: AI-availability x zone-membership predicting actual-AI-use, with zone-membership as binary moderator. Significant interaction = effective zone-demarcation.
