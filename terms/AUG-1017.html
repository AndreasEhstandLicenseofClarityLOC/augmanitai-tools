Gentle Layer (AUG-1017)

DE: Sanfte Schicht

Eine zusätzliche emotionale oder sprachliche Ebene, die Härte puffert und Kommunikation verträglicher macht, ohne Inhalte zu verzerren.

Operational Definition

Gentle Layer is observed when AI consistently adds a soft framing or hedged modulation to potentially harsh content, evidenced by detectable softeners (hedges, qualifiers, acknowledgments) wrapping critical statements without diluting their substance.

Measurement Schema

Tolerance of Ambiguity Scale administered alongside automated softener-density analysis (hedges, qualifiers, modal verbs per 100 words) on AI outputs. Gentle-Layer index = softener-density x participant tolerance-of-ambiguity score.
