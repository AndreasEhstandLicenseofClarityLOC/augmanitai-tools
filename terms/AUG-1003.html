Word Hunt (AUG-1003)

DE: Wort-Jagd

Der kollaborative Suchprozess nach der einen Formulierung, die Bedeutung, Ton und Kontext exakt trifft. Entscheidend ist das iterative Nachschärfen.

Operational Definition

Word Hunt is observed when participants engage in playful AI-mediated lexical-exploration sessions seeking targeted vocabulary (rare words, technical terms, evocative synonyms), characterised by iterative narrowing prompts.

Measurement Schema

Working memory span (Daneman-Carpenter reading span) administered pre- and post-engagement period, paired with vocabulary-retention assessment on hunted-words at 7-day follow-up. Word-Hunt benefit = retention x span improvement composite.

Inclusion criteria

At least 30 hunted-words AND completed working-memory assessment at both timepoints.

Exclusion criteria

Participants whose hunt-targets duplicate existing vocabulary in baseline assessment.
