Loop Laugh (AUG-1004)

DE: Loop-Lachen

Ein selbstverstärkender Humor-Loop zwischen Mensch und KI, der Spannung abbaut und Fehler entdramatisiert. Funktional, nicht kindisch.

Operational Definition

A Loop Laugh is recorded when both partners (human + AI assistant) produce humor-elements within two consecutive turns AND the human signals levity through at least one observable cue: laugh-emoji, 'haha'/'lol'-tokens, an explicit laughter-cue ('that's funny'), or a positive sentiment-shift greater than 0.3 in the next turn. One-sided humor without reciprocal acknowledgment is excluded.

Measurement Schema

Event-count per session normalized per 10 turns, complemented by a 7-point Likert post-session rating of perceived conversational lightness. Inter-rater agreement on humor-event coding measured via Cohen's kappa across two trained raters.

Inclusion criteria

Both partners generate humor and the human signals levity within a 2-turn window.

Exclusion criteria

Sarcastic, hostile, or one-sided humor lacking reciprocal acknowledgment.
