The Scatter Spark (AUG-0041)

The phenomenon whereby seemingly randomly scattered AI outputs — including those not directly matching the query — trigger unexpected associations and idea connections in the user. Describes the creative value of "noise" in AI interactions. Related to AUG-0031 (Semantic Spark) and AUG-0070 (The Surprise Field).

Operational Definition

The Scatter Spark is observed when a single AI prompt-response triggers multiple parallel idea-threads in the participant's subsequent output, evidenced by branching divergence from one anchor to multiple downstream concept-clusters.

Measurement Schema

t-SNE / UMAP embedding-distance applied to subsequent idea-utterances: scatter detected when post-trigger utterances form >= 3 distinct embedding-clusters at silhouette score > 0.4, with each cluster contributing >= 15% of utterances.
