The Wiki Wormhole (AUG-0520)

The phenomenon where a simple AI question leads to a chain of follow-up questions that pulls the user deep into a topic — comparable to the "Wikipedia hole" but AI-driven. Related to AUG-0342 (The Curiosity Loop), AUG-0413 (The Infinite Scroll), and AUG-0458 (The Curiosity Drill).

Operational Definition

The Wiki Wormhole is observed when AI-mediated exploration of a starting topic cascades into a multi-hop tangential exploration spanning more than 4 topical hops within one session, measured by topic-graph traversal depth.

Measurement Schema

Lin similarity / Wu-Palmer semantic similarity computed pairwise between consecutive turn-topics using WordNet hierarchy. Wormhole detected when at least 4 consecutive hops register pairwise similarity in the 0.2-0.5 range (semantically related but not identical).

Inclusion criteria

Session with at least 5 distinct topical anchors detectable via topic-model.

Exclusion criteria

Sessions where the user returns to the original topic after each tangent.
