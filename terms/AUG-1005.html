Pause Power

The user's practice of deliberately inserting a pause between receiving an AI output and sending the next input, in order to avoid impulsive replies and improve the quality of the following prompt. Distinguished from incidental delay by its intentional, regulatory character.

Operational Definition

STATE. The rated quantity is the user's deliberate inter-turn pausing: operationalised as the inter-turn interval (seconds between receiving an output and sending the next input) for turns the user marks or self-reports as intentional pauses, contrasted with their baseline interval. Higher relative interval on reflective turns indicates more of the construct.

Measurement Schema

Behavioural timing: log inter-turn latency per turn; compare median latency on user-flagged reflective turns against the user's overall median (within-subject ratio). Complementary self-report: a short habit scale (e.g. 1-7, 'I pause before replying') for trait-level pausing. Reported as reflective-turn latency ratio and habit-scale score.
