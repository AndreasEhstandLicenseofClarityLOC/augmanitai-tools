Algorithmic Intuition (AUG-0088)

The ability of experienced AI users to develop, through extended practice, a "sense" for which type of input produces which type of result — without having to consciously analyze this each time. Describes implicit knowledge about AI system behavior that forms through experience. Related to AUG-0133 (Prompt Craftsmanship), Phase 6 (Integrated Work State), and the System-Builder Profile (Profile 1).

Operational Definition

Algorithmic Intuition is recorded when a participant produces a correct or near-correct prediction of an AI system's output before viewing it, accompanied by a verbal rationale that references prior interaction patterns rather than guessing. Operationally instantiated as a pre-output prediction task with confidence rating.

Measurement Schema

Prediction-accuracy task: participants predict AI output on n=20 standardized prompts before reveal, scored by semantic-similarity (BERTScore) between prediction and actual output. Confidence reported on 5-point scale per item. Composite score = accuracy x confidence calibration.

Exclusion criteria
