Augmanitai (AUG-0001)

A framework concept describing the conscious, productive collaboration between humans and AI in everyday contexts. The term combines "augmentation" (enhancement) with "humanitai" (a neologism for human-centered AI use). Unlike the term "artificial intelligence," which centers the machine, Augmanitai describes exclusively the human perspective during collaboration. Anchor term for the corpus architecture; referenced across all 21 Axioms, 9 Dimensions, and 7 Phases.

Operational Definition

Augmanitai is observed at the corpus-level when sustained human-AI co-production generates terminology, frameworks, and shared artifacts across a defined longitudinal window, evidenced by terminology-uptake metrics and by spaced-repetition retention of co-produced concepts in participant recall tests.

Measurement Schema

Spaced-repetition retention curve: forgetting-curve fitted to recall-test scores at days 1, 7, 30, 90 for co-produced concepts vs solo-produced concepts. Half-life of retention reported per concept-class. Saccade-tracking eye-fixation on terminology in re-encounter task as secondary measure of recognition.

Inclusion criteria

Retention measured at all four time-points with co-produced half-life at least 1.5x solo-produced half-life.

Exclusion criteria

Concepts re-encountered between testing windows through external exposure (publications, news) that confound retention-attribution.
