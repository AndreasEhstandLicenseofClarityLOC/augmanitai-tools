Augmanitai

A superordinate framework term for the deliberate, productive collaboration between humans and AI in everyday contexts, viewed from the human side. It blends 'augmentation' with a coined 'humanitai' root and, unlike 'artificial intelligence', foregrounds the human's experience and agency rather than the machine.
