Calm Reset (AUG-1043)

DE: Ruhe-Reset

A brief pause and re-center after intense AI interaction, allowing energy to return before the next task. Distinct from extended rest — a short reset built into the work rhythm.

Operational Definition

Calm Reset is observed when a brief AI-mediated grounding exchange following a stress-event returns the participant's affective state toward baseline within 10 minutes, measured by valence and arousal indicators.

Measurement Schema

Affect Grid (valence-arousal 2D) administered at three timepoints: pre-stress, post-stress, post-reset. Reset effectiveness = (post-stress to post-reset Euclidean distance) / (post-stress to pre-stress Euclidean distance) on the 9x9 grid.
