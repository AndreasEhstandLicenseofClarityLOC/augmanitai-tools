The Accent Persistence (AUG-0711)

The observation that the phonetic patterns of a user's first language show through even in written AI inputs — sentence melody, word order, punctuation, and formulation patterns carry a "written accent." Related to AUG-0683 (The Origin Language), AUG-0691 (The Dialect Decoder), and AUG-0706 (The Mother Tongue Comfort).

Operational Definition

Accent Persistence is observed when stylistic features of a participant's prior writing (idiolect markers) persist in subsequent AI-assisted outputs, even when the AI is prompted toward a neutral style, indicating partial style-transfer resistance.

Measurement Schema

Stylometric distance via cosine similarity on character-n-gram (n=3,4,5) feature-vectors between participant's prior solo-writing and AI-assisted writing. Persistence = similarity > 0.7 above the random-baseline similarity computed across unrelated participant pairs.

Inclusion criteria

At least 5000 words of prior solo writing for stable stylometric baseline.

Exclusion criteria

Participants who explicitly request style-imitation of a different author.
