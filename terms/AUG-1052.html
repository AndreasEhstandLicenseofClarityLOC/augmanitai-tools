Backward Laugh (AUG-1052)

DE: Rückwärts-Lachen

Humor, der zeitversetzt wirkt: Erst versteht man den Witz, dann lacht man über die eigene Reaktion darauf.

Operational Definition

A Backward Laugh is recorded when a participant retrospectively expresses amusement about an earlier exchange (typically 5-50 turns prior) through phrases such as 'looking back that was funny', 'I just realized', or by explicitly referencing a past humorous moment. The trigger must reference a specific prior turn rather than general session impression.

Measurement Schema

Retrospective-reference coding scheme: count of backward humor-references per session, with mean lag-distance (number of turns between trigger and referenced moment). Coded by trained raters from full transcript with timestamp markers.

Inclusion criteria

Explicit retrospective reference to an earlier specific turn paired with amusement marker.

Exclusion criteria

Generic positive session-summary statements without reference to a specific earlier turn.
