The Calibrated Trust (AUG-0422)

DE: Das Kalibrierte Vertrauen

The user practice of matching AI-output confidence to verification effort: high-stakes outputs receive verification, routine outputs receive proportional checks. Trust is shaped by context rather than uniformly granted or withheld.

Operational Definition

Calibrated Trust is observed when a participant's expressed trust in specific AI outputs correlates with the AI's actual accuracy on those outputs across a verification window, measured by trust-accuracy calibration curves.

Measurement Schema

Brier score for participant trust-ratings (0-1) vs. ground-truth accuracy (0/1) across at least 50 outputs. Reliability and resolution components reported; calibration plotted with 10 equal-width bins.
