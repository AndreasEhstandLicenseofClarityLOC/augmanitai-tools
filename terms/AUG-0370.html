The Pet Lookup (AUG-0370)

The use of AI for quick information gathering on pet care questions — feeding, behavior, health prevention, species-appropriate husbandry. Describes a specific everyday application of Just-in-Time Competence (AUG-0043). Related to AUG-0251 (The Kitchen Table) and AUG-0216 (The Parenting Update).

Operational Definition

The Pet Lookup is observed when participants delegate to AI a recurring narrow factual lookup-pattern (named for the canonical example of looking up pet-care facts) characterised by repeated topical scope and minimal context-variation across instances.

Measurement Schema

Sequence alignment (Needleman-Wunsch algorithm) applied to query-strings within a single participant across 14 days, identifying high-alignment-score query-clusters as Pet-Lookup-patterns. Pattern-strength = alignment-score normalised by query-length.
