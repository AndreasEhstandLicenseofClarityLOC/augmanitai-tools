The Conversational Afterimage (AUG-0204)

The aftereffect of an intensive AI session in which the user adopts formulations, thinking structures, or argumentation patterns from the AI dialogue into their everyday communication — often unconsciously. Related to AUG-0046 (The Felt Echo), AUG-0003 (Fluide Identitätsmorphologie), and AUG-0125 (The Feedback Effect).

Operational Definition

The Conversational Afterimage is observed when conversational themes from prior sessions persist as residual framings in the participant's reasoning during a subsequent solo task, measured by lexical and conceptual carryover across an idle interval.

Measurement Schema

Cross-recurrence quantification analysis (CRQA) applied to lexical-feature vectors from prior conversation transcript and subsequent solo-task transcript. Afterimage-strength = recurrence-rate (RR) and determinism (DET) metrics across the cross-recurrence plot.

Inclusion criteria

Idle interval between 12 and 72 hours with both prior conversation and subsequent solo task available.

Exclusion criteria

Solo tasks where participant explicitly re-reads prior conversation during the task.
