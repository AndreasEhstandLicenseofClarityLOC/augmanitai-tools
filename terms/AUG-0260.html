The Plot Twist Partner (AUG-0260)

The use of AI as a creative sparring partner for narrative twists — in stories, presentations, pitches, or argumentation chains. Describes the AI's function as a supplier of unexpected plot changes and alternative storylines. Related to AUG-0235 (The Brainstorm Spark), AUG-0248 (The Surprise Angle), and the Experimenter Profile (Profile 4).

Operational Definition

The Plot Twist Partner is observed when the AI introduces unexpected narrative or framing shifts that the participant explicitly recognises as productive perturbation rather than disruption, indicated by a positive evaluation marker within two turns of the twist-event.

Measurement Schema

Information-theoretic surprisal (Shannon entropy) computed per AI-turn on the next-token-prediction distribution from a baseline LM, with twist-events flagged as surprisal > 95th percentile. Productive-twist rate = proportion of twist-events followed by positive evaluation markers within 2 turns.

Inclusion criteria

Surprisal > 95th-percentile AI-turn followed by positive evaluation marker within 2 turns.

Exclusion criteria

High-surprisal turns triggered by user topic-shift rather than AI-initiated reframing.
