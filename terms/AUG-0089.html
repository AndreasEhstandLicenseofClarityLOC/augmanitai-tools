The Pattern Sharpening (AUG-0089)

The effect whereby a user, through regular AI interaction, may correlate with improved ability to recognize patterns in data, texts, or arguments — the AI indirectly trains the human's pattern recognition. Describes a positive feedback effect of AI use on human analytical ability. Related to AUG-0054 (Augmented Understanding) and AUG-0088 (Algorithmic Intuition).

Operational Definition

The Pattern Sharpening is observed when iterative AI exchange reduces the noise-to-signal ratio in a participant's working representation of a recurring pattern, evidenced by progressive narrowing of the variance in pattern-defining features across successive articulations within one session.

Measurement Schema

Time-series autocorrelation function (ACF) computed on feature-vectors extracted per turn from the participant's pattern-description statements. Sharpening = monotonic decrease in feature-variance with positive lag-1 autocorrelation rho >= 0.6 indicating coherent narrowing.

Inclusion criteria

At least 8 successive pattern-articulations with measurable feature-variance decrease.

Exclusion criteria

Sessions where the pattern is reframed mid-stream (topic-shift breaking the autocorrelation series).
