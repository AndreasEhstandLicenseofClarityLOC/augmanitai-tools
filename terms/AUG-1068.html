End-of-Day High (AUG-1068)

DE: Tagesabschluss-Hoch

Ein positiver emotionaler Abschluss, der den Tag abrundet und Integration ermöglicht.

Operational Definition

Operational scope: an instance of 'End-of-Day High' is counted when the observable pattern described above (Ein positiver emotionaler Abschluss, der den Tag abrundet und Integration ermögl...) occurs at least once within a defined interaction segment of N turns (typically N=10), as coded by trained raters.

Measurement Schema

Self-report Likert (1-7) on perceived humor-loop strength after a session; complementary indicator: laughter-event count per minute.
