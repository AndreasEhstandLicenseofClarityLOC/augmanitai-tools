The Rhetorical Tone Detector (AUG-0670)

The individual ability of a user to recognize and classify the rhetorical tone of an AI output — whether the AI formulates seriously, ironically, neutrally, or exaggeratingly. This ability varies strongly between users. Related to AUG-0669 (The Rhetorical Style Differential), AUG-0668 (The Humor Portability), and AUG-0451 (The Token Awareness).

Operational Definition

Rhetorical Tone Detection is recorded when a participant correctly identifies the rhetorical register (e.g., ironic, hedging, declarative, evasive, conciliatory) of an AI utterance from a closed-set of labels, with optional free-text justification. Detection is assessed on standardized stimulus passages.

Measurement Schema

Forced-choice classification task on n=30 AI-generated passages across 6 tone-categories. Accuracy reported as proportion correct; sensitivity/specificity per category. Free-text justifications coded for evidence-quality on a 3-point rubric (cue-based / partial / unsupported).
