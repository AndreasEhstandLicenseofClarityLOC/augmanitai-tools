The Duplicate Notice (AUG-0639)

The user's recognition that an AI response is substantively a repetition of an earlier response — differently formulated but identical in core. Related to AUG-0560 (The Conversation Loop), AUG-0383 (The Context Collapse), and AUG-0088 (Algorithmic Intuition).

Operational Definition

The Duplicate Notice is observed when participants explicitly flag AI output as substantively similar to previously received output (within or across sessions), characterised by an explicit duplicate-marker phrase and a flagged-output identifier.

Measurement Schema

Cohen-Yates chi-square test (with Yates correction for 2x2) on flagged-duplicate counts vs. expected-by-chance duplicates across n participants. Duplicate-notice rate per participant compared via chi-square goodness-of-fit.
