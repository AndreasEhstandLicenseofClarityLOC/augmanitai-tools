The Wall Check (AUG-0345)

The moment a user encounters the limits of an AI system — the AI cannot solve the task, provides faulty information, or reveals comprehension limits. Describes the productive experience of learning a system's limitations. Related to Axiom 9 (Productive Skepticism), AUG-0177 (The Trust Setting), and AUG-0176 (The Capability Discovery).

Operational Definition

The Wall Check is observed when participants use AI to probe whether a proposed approach will encounter known constraints or hard limits, characterised by explicit constraint-enumeration prompts followed by constraint-validation responses.

Measurement Schema

Kullback-Leibler divergence between the participant's pre-check probability-distribution over feasibility-outcomes and the post-check distribution, elicited via brief 5-item feasibility-rating instrument. Larger KL = larger informational update from the wall-check.
