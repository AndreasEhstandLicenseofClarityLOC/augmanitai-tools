The Dinner Shortcut (AUG-0435)

The quick AI query for everyday food decisions — "What can I cook with these ingredients?", "What fits as a side dish?" Describes the lowest-threshold form of AI use in the household. Related to AUG-0266 (The Recipe Riff), AUG-0251 (The Kitchen Table), and AUG-0373 (The Quick Check).

Operational Definition

The Dinner Shortcut is observed when participants employ AI for time-bounded everyday-decision tasks (named for the canonical evening meal-planning case) characterised by short session-duration (< 5 min) and immediate actionable outcome.

Measurement Schema

Latent class analysis on session-feature vectors (duration, turn-count, decision-explicit-output, time-of-day) to identify Dinner-Shortcut class. Class-membership probabilities reported per session; class characterised by short-duration + actionable-output + evening-cluster.
