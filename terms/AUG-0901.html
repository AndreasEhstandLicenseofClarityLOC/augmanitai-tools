The Emergent Coordination (AUG-0901)

The observation that in multi-agent systems, coordination patterns can emerge that were not explicitly programmed — the interaction of subsystems produces behavior not intended in the individual systems. Related to AUG-0900 (The Distributed Coordination), AUG-0948 (The Scope Creep Alert), and AUG-0949 (The Unintended Action).

Operational Definition

Emergent Coordination is observed when two or more participants converge on synchronised problem-decomposition strategies during multi-agent AI-mediated work, without explicit coordination instructions, evidenced by aligned task-segmentation across independent agents.

Measurement Schema

Network modularity computed via the Louvain method on the joint task-graph (nodes = sub-tasks, edges = shared dependencies). Coordination-strength = modularity score Q; values Q > 0.3 indicate non-trivial clustering attributable to emergent coordination.

Inclusion criteria

Multi-agent session with at least 3 participants and >= 15 sub-tasks.

Exclusion criteria

Sessions with explicit pre-assigned task-segmentation.
