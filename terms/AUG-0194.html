The Positive Surprise (AUG-0194)

An AI response that positively exceeds the user's expectations in quality, depth, or perspective. Describes the moment when the AI delivers more than the user expected — thereby increasing trust in the collaboration. Related to AUG-0070 (The Surprise Field), AUG-0031 (Semantic Spark), and AUG-0177 (The Trust Setting).

Operational Definition

The Positive Surprise is observed when AI output exceeds the participant's pre-stated expectation across at least one dimension (depth, relevance, creativity), evidenced by explicit surprise-markers paired with positive evaluation within two turns.

Measurement Schema

Eye-tracking dwell-time per AOI (Area of Interest) on AI-output text-regions immediately after reveal, with surprise-events flagged as dwell-time exceeding baseline + 1.5 SD AND co-occurring with verbal surprise-marker. Positive Surprise rate = events where evaluation marker is positive / total surprise-events.
