Giggle Chain (AUG-1051)

DE: Kicher-Kette

Eine sich selbst verstärkende Abfolge humorvoller Reaktionen, die Leichtigkeit erzeugt und kognitive Blockaden löst.

Operational Definition

A Giggle Chain is observed when a cascade of humor-events occurs across three or more consecutive turns in an AI-mediated session, each turn containing a humor-marker and each contributing escalating or transforming the prior humor-element.

Measurement Schema

Sequence pattern mining (PrefixSpan algorithm) over humor-marker token sequences identifying chains of length >= 3. Chain-quality = chain length x escalation-coherence rating (1-5 by trained coders).

Inclusion criteria

At least 3 consecutive humor-marked turns within 5-turn window.

Exclusion criteria

Chains broken by topic-shift or single-side humor maintenance.
