The Echo Courage (AUG-0047)

The phenomenon that a user, after a confirming AI interaction, gains more confidence to defend an idea before other people. The AI serves as an initial sounding board: if the idea holds there, the user is more likely to carry it into the real world. Related to AUG-0166 (The Borrowed Confidence) and AUG-0232 (The Courage Click).

Operational Definition

Echo Courage is observed when AI-acknowledgment of a participant's tentative position enables a subsequent more-committed restatement of that position, measured as a confidence-uplift between initial tentative articulation and committed restatement.

Measurement Schema

Bootstrap confidence intervals on the effect-size (Cohen's d) of the confidence-shift between tentative and committed statements, computed with 1000 resamples per participant. Significance reported as 95% CI excluding zero.

Inclusion criteria

At least one tentative statement followed by committed restatement after AI-acknowledgment.

Exclusion criteria

Statements where AI provides new information rather than acknowledgment.
