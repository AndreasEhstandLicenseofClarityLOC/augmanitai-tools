Relaxation Breath (AUG-1011)

DE: Entspannungs-Atem

Eine kurze, angeleitete Atem- oder Aufmerksamkeitsintervention durch die KI, um Stress zu senken oder einen Übergang zwischen kognitiven Zuständen zu ermöglichen.

Operational Definition

Relaxation Breath is observed when an AI-guided brief breathing-pacing interaction produces measurable respiratory-rate slowing and vagal-tone change in the participant within a 4-minute exposure window.

Measurement Schema

Polyvagal Theory ventral-vagal indices computed from respiratory-sinus-arrhythmia (RSA) recording: RSA-amplitude pre and post AI-guided session. Relaxation Breath confirmed when RSA-amplitude increase >= 0.5 ln(ms^2) AND respiratory rate decrease >= 3 breaths/min.

Inclusion criteria

Concurrent respiratory and ECG recording for RSA computation across pre/post windows.

Exclusion criteria
