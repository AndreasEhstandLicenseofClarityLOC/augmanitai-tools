The Gesture Language (AUG-0918)

The communication between humans and embodied AI systems through gestures — hand signals, posture, movements that the system recognizes and interprets. Related to AUG-0917 (The Touch Interface), AUG-0919 (The Spatial Awareness), and AUG-0914 (The Physical Presence).

Operational Definition

The Gesture Language is observed in video-recorded AI-mediated sessions when participants develop reproducible non-verbal gestures (pointing, swiping, drawing-air-paths) accompanying their verbal AI-interaction, often co-occurring with spatial-reasoning content.

Measurement Schema

Action Identification Theory (AIT) level coding applied to gesture-verb pairs: each gesture is coded as low-level (concrete motor-act) or high-level (abstract intentional-act). Gesture-language richness = high-level proportion x gesture-vocabulary size.

Inclusion criteria

Video-recorded session with at least 20 codable gesture events.

Exclusion criteria

Sessions where gestures are confined to keyboard or device-input only.
