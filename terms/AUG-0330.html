The Source Curiosity (AUG-0330)

DE: Die Quellen-Neugier

The user's interest in tracing the origin and grounding of AI-generated statements — a productive curiosity about where information comes from, supporting informed evaluation of AI outputs.

Operational Definition

The Source Curiosity pattern is observed when participants probe the provenance of AI-stated facts, characterised by source-asking prompts and downstream engagement with the sources provided.

Measurement Schema

Curiosity and Exploration Inventory (CEI-II) administered alongside log-analysis of source-asking prompts per session. Source-Curiosity = CEI-II stretching subscale x source-asking-rate per session.

Inclusion criteria

CEI-II completed within 30 days of session-log window AND at least 20 sessions logged.

Exclusion criteria

Sessions where AI is configured to suppress source-provision regardless of prompt.
