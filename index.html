English

From Court to Code to Connection. As a tennis coach and educator with 25 years of professional sports coaching — from club players to Bundesliga level — and 6 years of focused work on mental experiences in competitive sport, Andreas Ehstand noticed something familiar when he began working intensively with AI in 2025: the same felt moments he had spent decades learning to recognize on the tennis court and in the classroom were happening in human-AI interaction. So he did what 25 years of coaching and teaching had taught him — he observed the experiences and started naming them. The result is AUGMANITAI: an open, DOI-archived compendium of 2,000+ named phenomena for human experiences in AI interaction. New words for new worlds. Stay human.

Deutsch

Vom Tennisplatz zur KI. Als Tennistrainer und Pädagoge mit 25 Jahren professionellem Sportcoaching — vom Breitensport bis zur Bundesliga — und 6 Jahren Fokus auf mentale Erfahrungen im Leistungssport bemerkte Andreas Ehstand beim intensiven Arbeiten mit KI ab 2025 etwas Vertrautes: dieselben gefühlten Momente, die er über Jahrzehnte auf dem Tennisplatz und im Klassenzimmer zu erkennen gelernt hatte, traten in der Mensch-KI-Interaktion auf. Also tat er das, was ihn 25 Jahre Coaching und Unterrichten gelehrt hatten — er beobachtete die Erfahrungen und begann sie zu benennen. Das Ergebnis ist AUGMANITAI: ein offenes, DOI-archiviertes Compendium mit über 1.000 Begriffen für menschliche Erlebnisse in der KI-Interaktion. Neue Worte für neue Welten. Stay human.

Who finds this useful

  • Researchers in human-AI interaction
    A starting vocabulary for phenomena that have not yet been named — extensible under CC BY 4.0 attribution.
  • Decision-makers under cognitive load
    Observable patterns to recognize when AI-augmented work is sharpening, drifting, or eroding judgement.
  • Educators & methodology designers
    A reference taxonomy for teaching how humans actually interact with generative AI in real practice.
  • Methodology researchers
    A cross-domain transfer of high-performance sport-science analysis (observable, decomposable, measurable, controllable) into human-AI interaction phenomenology.

Living document — readers are invited to extend, contest, or rebuild this vocabulary against their own observations. The terms here are one researcher's snapshot, not a definitive map.
